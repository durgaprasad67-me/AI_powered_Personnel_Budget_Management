FinSight AI budgeting guide

Start with take-home income and assign every rupee a purpose. Separate fixed
needs such as housing and utilities from flexible spending such as dining,
shopping, and entertainment. A useful starting point is to keep essential
expenses below 50 percent of income, but personal circumstances should guide
the final numbers.

Review food and transport spending weekly because small purchases can add up.
Set a category budget before the month begins, then compare actual spending
with the budget at least once a week. If a category is over budget, reduce
optional spending before cutting essential bills.

Build an emergency fund gradually. Keep the fund in an accessible account and
target three to six months of essential expenses. Automate a transfer after
each payday so saving happens before discretionary spending.

Invest only after essential bills and high-interest debt payments are covered.
Record investments separately from ordinary expenses, and review recurring
investments when income or goals change.

For financial goals, record a target amount, current progress, and target date.
Divide the remaining amount by the number of months left to estimate a monthly
contribution. Revisit the estimate whenever the target date or income changes.
